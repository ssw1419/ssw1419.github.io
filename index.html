Building safer, more reliable AI.

I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence at Korea University. My research focuses on the safety and alignment of large language models, with broader interests in reasoning and sequential decision making.

I previously worked with Fangzhao Wu at Microsoft Research Asia and Meeyoung Cha at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy. I am also interested in extending language model safety research to AI agents and embodied intelligence.
